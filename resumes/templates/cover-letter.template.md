# [Your Name] - [Target Role]

[Public contact link]<br>
[City, Country or Remote]<br>
[Date]

Dear [hiring manager or team],

Open with the specific role and one clear reason the organization's problem or mission interests
you. Avoid generic enthusiasm that could apply to any company.

Connect one requirement from the job description to a verified example from your experience. State
the situation, what you did, and the outcome in three or four concise sentences. Add a second
example only if it demonstrates a different important requirement.

Explain how you would apply that evidence to the team's current needs. Keep the claim proportionate
to what you know; do not pretend to have private knowledge of the organization.

Close with a brief invitation to discuss the work. Thank the reader without repeating your resume.

Sincerely,

[Your Name]
